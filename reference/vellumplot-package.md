# vellumplot: A Grammar of Graphics on the 'vellum' Backend

A declarative, pipe-first grammar of graphics that compiles an
inspectable plot specification into a 'vellum' scene and renders it. A
plot is built up as a serializable spec (data, layers, scales) and
nothing is drawn until it is compiled: encodings are resolved, scales
are trained, a panel layout is measured, and guides and marks are
emitted as 'vellum' grobs.
